JumpHMM.HybridSingleIndexModel — Type
HybridSingleIndexModel <: AbstractDependenceModel

Variance-corrected Single-Index Model with per-ticker HMM marginals and copula dependence. Combines:

  • SIM factor structure: G_i = αᵢ + βᵢ × G_market + εᵢ
  • Per-ticker HMM marginals for idiosyncratic draws (preserves tails, regimes)
  • Variance correction so Var[G_i] ≈ Var[marginal_i] (marginal-preserving branch) or exact R² recovery (R²-preserving branch for high-R² ETFs)
  • β clipping with an idiosyncratic floor to prevent degenerate paths
  • Copula rank-reordering to preserve cross-sectional dependence

See sample_dependence for the full construction recipe.

source
JumpHMM.SectorFactorModel — Type
SectorFactorModel <: AbstractDependenceModel

Multi-asset composition with one market model, one model per sector, and one model per name: gi(t) = αi + βi(t)·gm(t) + δi·fs(t) + si·ε̃i(t) Names are linked only through the shared market draw g_m and sector draw f_s on the same day (no copula). α and β come from the single-index fit; β is fixed unless β_scale is nonzero, in which case it drifts as an exponential moving average of normal shocks with half-life β_halflife. See fit(SectorFactorModel, ...) and sample_dependence(::SectorFactorModel, n; seed).

JumpHMM.fit — Method
fit(::Type{HybridSingleIndexModel}, tickers, prices, market_ticker; kwargs...) → HybridSingleIndexModel

Fit a variance-corrected hybrid SIM end-to-end from a price matrix. This:

  1. Fits a JumpHMM to the market column
  2. Fits per-ticker HMM marginals for all non-market tickers
  3. Runs OLS regression per ticker against the market → α, β, r²
  4. Fits a copula on the OLS residuals

The resulting model can be passed to sample_dependence to generate synthetic growth rates via the hybrid construction (variance correction + copula reorder).

Arguments

  • tickers — column labels for prices, must include market_ticker
  • prices — (n_obs × n_assets) price matrix
  • market_ticker — which ticker is the market index (e.g. "SPY")
  • copula_type — dependence model for residuals (default StudentTCopula)
  • rf, N, ν, dt, min_obs — passed through to fit(JumpHiddenMarkovModel, ...)
  • r2_preserve_threshold — R² cutoff for the R²-preserving branch (default 0.80)
  • idiosyncratic_floor — minimum idiosyncratic variance share f (default 0.10)
source
JumpHMM.fit — Method
fit(::Type{SectorFactorModel}, tickers, prices, market, sector; kwargs...) → (model, marginals)

Fit a SectorFactorModel. tickers and the columns of prices include the market ticker market; sector holds one label per non-market ticker, in the order those tickers appear in tickers. Every sector needs at least two names.

Steps: growth rates G = excess_growth_rates(prices; rf, dt); single-index OLS of each name on the market → α̂, β̂, r², residuals E; sector_factors and sector_loadings of E; intercepts α = ᾱ + intercept_pull·(α̂ − ᾱ); a Jump-HMM for the market (its prices), for each name (its prices), and for each sector (the compounded sector factor, 100·exp(cumsum(F·dt)), fit with rf = 0). Variance targets are the real variances. ε_center[i] is the mean of one center_steps-day draw of name model i seeded with center_seed + i.

Keywords: rf=0.0, N=100, ν=5.0, dt=1/252, min_obs=2 (as for JumpHiddenMarkovModel); intercept_pull=1.0 (1 keeps α̂, 0 sets every α to the mean); β_halflife=126.0 (trading days); β_scale=nothing (zeros: fixed β); floor=0.10; r2_threshold=0.80; center_steps=1_000_000; center_seed=4·10^12 (far from every path seed, as in the reference). market_model, name_models (one per name, in name order) and sector_models (one per sector, in sorted-label order) replace the models fit would fit, for a caller that fits or tunes them its own way; ε_center always comes from the name models actually stored. A wrong length throws ArgumentError.

Returns the model and a Dict of the name models keyed by ticker.

JumpHMM.sample_dependence — Method
sample_dependence(model::HybridSingleIndexModel, n) → Matrix{Float64}

Generate n growth-rate observations for each ticker via the hybrid SIM recipe:

  1. Simulate a market path from the market HMM
  2. For each ticker, draw an idiosyncratic path from its HMM marginal
  3. Apply variance correction (R²-preserving or marginal-preserving branch)
  4. Copula rank-reorder the scaled innovations
  5. Compose: G_i = α_i + β_i · G_market + ε_i

Returns an (n_eff × n_assets) matrix of annualized growth rates, where n_eff = n - 1 (first observation trimmed for alignment with the HMM simulation convention).

Branches

  • R²-preserving (r² ≥ r2_preserve_threshold): scales ε so synthetic R² matches calibrated R² exactly. SPY-like tickers with R²≈1 produce ε≡0.
  • Marginal-preserving (r² < threshold): scales ε to preserve the HMM marginal variance, with β clipping when the market loading exceeds 1 - idiosyncratic_floor of the generator variance.
source
JumpHMM.sample_dependence — Method
sample_dependence(model::SectorFactorModel, n; seed) → (g_m, m_states, jumps, F, G, β_eff, n_clipped, name_jumps)

Draw one n-day synthetic path of the whole universe: compose(model, sample_draws(model, n; seed)). g_m, m_states, jumps are the market draw (growth, states, jump-episode flags); F (n × sectors) holds one draw of each sector model; G (n × names) the composed name growth; β_eff (n × names) each name's per-day β after any clipping; n_clipped (one per name) the number of days whose loadings were shrunk (0 or n, see compose_name); name_jumps (n × names) marks the days any of a name's components (market, sector, own) was in a jump episode. Seeds as in sample_draws; the same seed reproduces the path exactly, and the output is the same as in version 0.2.0.

source
JumpHMM.sample_draws — Function
sample_draws(model::SectorFactorModel, n; seed) → (g_m, m_states, jumps, F, sector_jumps, E, own_jumps, Z)

Every random component of one n-day path, before composition: the market draw (g_m, m_states, jumps), one draw of each sector model (F, n × sectors, and sector_jumps), one draw of each name model (E, n × names, and own_jumps), and the standard normal β-drift shocks Z (n × names). Seeds: market seed; sector s 10^12 + 10^4·seed + s; name j 2·10^12 + 10^4·seed + j; β-drift shocks from Xoshiro(3·10^12 + seed). The streams stay distinct for 0 ≤ seed < 10^8 and fewer than 10^4 names and sectors; outside that range ArgumentError is thrown. Like simulate(::JumpHiddenMarkovModel, ...), this reseeds the global RNG.

compose(model, sample_draws(model, n; seed)) is the path sample_dependence(model, n; seed) draws. Keeping the draws lets a caller recompose the same randomness under other parameters (with_parameters), for example to fit the β drift.

source
JumpHMM.compose — Function
compose(model::SectorFactorModel, draws) → (G, β_eff, n_clipped, name_jumps)

Compose the name growth rates of one path from its draws (sample_draws) with the model's α, β, β drift (β_halflife, β_scale), loadings and variance targets: G (n × names, yr⁻¹), β_eff (each name's per-day β after any clipping), n_clipped (one per name, 0 or n, see compose_name) and name_jumps (days any of a name's components, market, sector or own, was in a jump episode). Deterministic: the same draws and model give the same output.

Sector Factor Model Internals

JumpHMM.ema_rate — Function
ema_rate(h) → Float64

Weight a = 1 − 2^(−1/h) of an exponential moving average with half-life h (trading days). Throws ArgumentError if h ≤ 0.

source
JumpHMM.drift_path — Function
drift_path(z, h, k) → Vector{Float64}

Exponential moving average of the standard-normal shocks z, scaled by k, with half-life h (trading days). With a = ema_rate(h): x₁ = k·√(a/(2−a))·z₁ and xₜ = (1−a)·xₜ₋₁ + a·k·zₜ. The start is drawn from the stationary distribution, so every xₜ has variance drift_variance(h, k). The output is linear in k.

source
JumpHMM.drift_variance — Function
drift_variance(h, k) → Float64

Stationary variance k²·a/(2−a) of drift_path with half-life h and scale k, where a = ema_rate(h).

source

sector_factors(E, sector) → (sectors, F)

Equal-weight sector factors from the residual matrix E (days × names). sectors holds the sorted unique labels of sector (one label per column of E), and column s of F (days × sectors) is the mean of the columns of E whose label is sectors[s].

source
JumpHMM.loo_loadings — Function
loo_loadings(E, sector) → Vector{Float64}

Leave-one-out sector loadings: δᵢ is the OLS slope, with an intercept, of column i of E on the mean of the other columns in the same sector. Leaving column i out keeps its own noise out of the regressor. Throws ArgumentError for a sector with one name.

source
JumpHMM.sector_loadings — Function
sector_loadings(E, sector) → Vector{Float64}

Sector loadings δᵢ for the generator. Each sector's loo_loadings are multiplied by one scale κ, chosen so that the shared co-movement the generator builds, δᵢ·δⱼ·var(f_s) averaged over the pairs i ≠ j in the sector, equals the mean pairwise covariance of the columns of E in that sector. f_s is the full equal-weight sector mean (sector_factors). Throws ArgumentError for a sector with one name, or where either mean pairwise covariance is not positive (the message names the sector).

source
JumpHMM.compose_name — Function
compose_name(α, β, δ, g_m, f, ε, σ²_m, σ²_s, σ²_gen, r²; ε_center, β²_mean,
             floor=0.10, r2_threshold=0.80) → (g, β_eff, n_clipped)

Compose one name's daily growth g[t] = α[t] + c·β[t]·g_m[t] + c·δ·f[t] + √s²·(ε[t] − ε_center). α and β are per-day paths, δ the sector loading, and g_m, f, ε the market, sector, and own draws. σ²_m, σ²_s, σ²_gen are the fixed variances of the market growth, the sector factor, and the name's own growth; r² is the name's real single-index R²; β²_mean is the long-run mean of β[t]².

The variance split is fixed for the whole path: v̄ = β²_mean·σ²_m + δ²·σ²_s and ρ̄ = v̄/σ²_gen. If r² ≥ r2_threshold, s² = v̄(1 − r²)/r²/σ²_gen and c = 1 (keep the real R²). Otherwise, if ρ̄ ≤ 1 − floor, s² = 1 − ρ̄ and c = 1 (match the name's variance). Otherwise both loadings shrink by c = √((1 − floor)/ρ̄), s² = floor, and every day counts as clipped. Returns the growth path g, the per-day β_eff = c·β, and n_clipped (0 or the path length).
